Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Welcome to Expense Tracker: Money Manager by TR Tech Solutions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your financial information is safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: Name, Email Address, and Phone Number when creating an account for authentication.

Financial Data: Income/Expense records, budget categories, notes, and timestamps saved securely via Google Firebase Firestore.

Security Data: Local biometric state (processed strictly on device via Android BiometricPrompt).

Our application uses Google AdMob to deliver ads. AdMob may utilize non-personal device advertising identifiers in compliance with Google policies. Users upgrading to our Pro Plan enjoy a completely Ad-Free experience.

3. Contact Developer

For any privacy inquiries: rplaystore2026@gmail.com

User Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users can delete their account and associated data anytime.

How to Delete Within the App:

  1. Open the Expense Tracker application.
  2. Navigate to the Profile / Settings screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm deletion. Your authentication credentials and all Firestore transactions will be permanently purged immediately.

Manual Request:

You can also send a deletion request to rplaystore2026@gmail.com with your registered email ID. Data is removed within 48 hours.
